<objective>
Create the benchmark script and test infrastructure, then evaluate all 10 SegFormer checkpoints on the demo video to identify the checkpoint with the best temporal stability. Update MODEL_DIR in config.py to point to the winning checkpoint.

Purpose: The current model may not be the most temporally stable. Checkpoint-500 through checkpoint-5000 span the training trajectory and earlier checkpoints may generalize better (less overfitting = less frame-to-frame instability). We cannot tune smoothing without first knowing which raw checkpoint is most stable — garbage in, garbage out.
